[The 8{sup}`th` book of The Bible](#RUTH)

(INTRO_RUTH)=
This idyllic tale concerning the time of the judges tells of the devotion of a Moabite woman who cast in her lot with Israel after the death of her Israelite husband. She became, by a second marriage, to a kinsman of her former spouse, the great-grandmother of King David (see caption here).
