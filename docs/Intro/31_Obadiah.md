(INTRO_OBADIAH)=
Comprising only one chapter, the Book of Obadiah has but a single theme: the approaching judgement of God on the Edomites for their treachery toward their brothers, the Israelites, during the Babylonian Attack on Jerusalem (see {ref}`here<fig:Petra_Treasury>`).
