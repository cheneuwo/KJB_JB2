{ref}`The 37th book of The Bible<HAGGAI>`

(INTRO_HAGGAI)=
A collection of four addresses, all delivered in a short space of time in 520 B.C., that stimulated the returned exiles to rebuild the ruined Temple by telling of how the restored Temple will glorify God.
