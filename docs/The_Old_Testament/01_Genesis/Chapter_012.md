# CHAPTER TWELVE, GENESIS

:::{div}
:class: paragraph-title
God's call to Abram
:::

(GENESIS_12_1)=
1. Now the {span .divine-name}`LORD` had said unto {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father's house, unto a land that I will show thee:
1. and I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing:
1. and I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that cruseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.
1. So {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` departed, as the {span .divine-name}`LORD` had spoken unto him; and {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` went with him: and {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` *was* seventy and five years old when he departed out of [Haran](wiki:Harran_(biblical_place)).
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` took {ref}`Sarai<WHO_Sarai>` his wife, and {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` his brother's son, and all their substance they had gathered, and the souls that they had gotten in [Haran](wiki:Harran_(biblical_place)); and they went forth to go into the [land of Canaan](wiki:Canaan); and into the [land of Canaan](wiki:Canaan) they came.
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` pass through the land unto the place of [Sichem](wiki:Shechem), unto the plain of [Moreh](wiki:Givat_HaMoreh). And the [Canaanite](wiki:Canaan) *was* then in the land.
1. And the {span .divine-name}`LORD` appeared unto {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`, and said, Unto they seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the {span .divine-name}`LORD`, who appeared unto him.
1. And he removed from thence unto a mountain on the east of [Beth-el](wiki:Bethel), and pitched his tent, *having* [Beth-el](wiki:Bethel) on the west, and [Ha'i](wiki:Ai_(Canaan)) on the east: and there he builded an altar unto the {span .divine-name}`LORD`, and called upon the name of the {span .divine-name}`LORD`.
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` journeyed, going on still toward the south.

Abram in Egypt

(GENESIS_12_10)=
10. And there was a famine in the land: and {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` went down into [Egypt](wiki:Biblical_Egypt) to sojourn there; for the famine *was* grievous in the land.
1. And it came to pass, when he was come near to enter into [Egypt](wiki:Biblical_Egypt), that he said unto {ref}`Sarai<WHO_Sarai>` his wife, Behold now, I know that thou *art* a fair woman to look upon:
1. therefore it shall come to pass, when the Egyptians shall see thee, that they shall say, This *is* his wife: and they will kill me, but they shall save thee alive.
1. Say, I pray thee, thou *art* my sister: that it may be well with me for thy sake; and my soul shall live because of thee.
1. And it came to pass, that, when {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` was come into [Egypt](wiki:Biblical_Egypt), the Egyptians beheld the woman that she *was* very fair.
1. The princes also of [Pharaoh](wiki:Pharaohs_in_the_Bible) saw her, and commended her before [Pharaoh](wiki:Pharaohs_in_the_Bible): and the woman was taken into Pharaoh's house.
1. And he entreated {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` well for her sake; and he had sheep, and oxen, and he asses, and menservants, and maidservants, and she asses, and camels.
1. And the {span .divine-name}`LORD` plagued [Pharaoh](wiki:Pharaohs_in_the_Bible) and his house with great plagues, because of {ref}`Sarai<WHO_Sarai>`, {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`'s wife.
1. And [Pharaoh](wiki:Pharaohs_in_the_Bible) called {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`, and said, What *is* this *that* hast done unto me? why didst thou not tell me that she *was* thy wife?
1. Why saidst thou, She *is* my sister? so I might have taken her to me to wife: now therefore behold thy wife, take *her*, and go thy way.
1. And [Pharaoh](wiki:Pharaohs_in_the_Bible) commanded *his* men concerning him: and they sent him away, and his wife, and all that he had.
