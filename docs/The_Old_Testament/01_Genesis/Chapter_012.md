1. Now the {span .divine-name}`LORD` had said unto {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father's house, unto a land that I will show thee:
1. and I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing:
1. and I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that cruseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.
1. So {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` departed, as the {span .divine-name}`LORD` had spoken unto him; and {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` went with him: and {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` *was* seventy and five years old when he departed out of [Haran](wiki:Harran_(biblical_place)).
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` took {ref}`Sarai<WHO_Sarai>` his wife, and {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` his brother's son, and all their substance they had gathered, and the souls that they had gotten in [Haran](wiki:Harran_(biblical_place)); and they went forth to go into the [land of Canaan](wiki:Canaan); and into the [land of Canaan](wiki:Canaan) they came.
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` pass through the land unto the place of [Sichem](wiki:Shechem), unto the plain of [Moreh](wiki:Givat_HaMoreh). And the [Canaanite](wiki:Canaan) *was* then in the land.
1. And the {span .divine-name}`LORD` appeared unto {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`, and said, Unto they seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the {span .divine-name}`LORD`, who appeared unto him.
1. And he removed from thence unto a mountain on the east of [Beth-el](wiki:Bethel), and pitched his tent, *having* [Beth-el](wiki:Bethel) on the west, and [Ha'i](wiki:Ai_(Canaan)) on the east: and there he builded an altar unto the {span .divine-name}`LORD`, and called upon the name of the {span .divine-name}`LORD`.
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` journeyed, goin on still toward the south.
