(GENESIS_14_1)=
1. And it came to pass in the days of {ref}`Am'raphel<WHO_Amraphel>` king of [Shinar](wiki:Shinar), {ref}`Ar'i-och<WHO_Arioch>` king of [Ella'sar](wiki:Arioch), {ref}`Chedorla-o'mer<WHO_Chedorlaomer>` king of [Elam](wiki:Elam), and {ref}`Tidal<WHO_Tidal>` king of nations;
(GENESIS_14_2)=
2. *that these* made war with {ref}`Bera<WHO_Bera>` king of [Sodom](wiki:Sodom_and_Gomorrah), and with {ref}`Birsha<WHO_Birsha>` king of [Gomorash](wiki:Sodom_and_Gomorrah), {ref}`Shinab<WHO_Shinab>` king of [Admah](wiki:Admah), and {ref}`Sheme'ber<WHO_Shemeber>` king of [Zeboi'im](wiki:Zeboim_(Hebrew_Bible)), and the king of [Bela](wiki:Zoara), which is {ref}`Zo'ar<WHO_Zoar>`.
1. All these were joined together in the vale of [Siddim](wiki:Battle_of_Siddim), which is the salt sea.
1. Twelve years they served {ref}`Chedorla-o'mer<WHO_Chedorlaomer>`, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.
1. And in the fourteenth year came {ref}`Chedorla-o'mer<WHO_Chedorlaomer>`, and the kings that *were* with him, and smote the [Reph'a-im](wiki:Rephaite) in [Ash'teroth-karna'im](wiki:Ashteroth_Karnaim), and the [Zuzim](wiki:Zuzim_(biblical_people)) in Ham, and the [Emin](wiki:Emite) in [Shaveh Kiri-atha'im](wiki:List_of_minor_biblical_places).
1. and the [Horites](wiki:Horites) in their mount [Se'ir](wiki:Mount_Seir), unto [El-pa'ran](wiki:Desert_of_Paran), which *is* by the wilderness.
1. And they returned, and came to Enmish'pat, which is [Kadesh](wiki:Kadesh_(Syria)), and smote all the country of the [Amal'ekites](wiki:Amalek), and also the [Amorites](wiki:Amorites), that dwelt in [Haz'ezon-ta'mar](wiki:Ein_Gedi_(archaeological_site)).
1. And there went out the King of [Sodom](wiki:Sodom_and_Gomorrah), and the king of [Gomorash](wiki:Sodom_and_Gomorrah), and the king of [Admah](wiki:Admah), and the king of [Zeboi'im](wiki:Zeboim_(Hebrew_Bible)), and the king of [Bela](wiki:Zoara), (the same *is* {ref}`Zo'ar<WHO_Zoar>`;) and they joined battle with them in the vale of [Siddim](wiki:Battle_of_Siddim);
1. with {ref}`Chedorla-o'mer<WHO_Chedorlaomer>` king of [Elam](wiki:Elam), and with {ref}`Tidal<WHO_Tidal>` king of nations, and {ref}`Am'raphel<WHO_Amraphel>` king of [Shinar](wiki:Shinar), and {ref}`Ar'i-och<WHO_Arioch>` king of [Ella'sar](wiki:Arioch); four kings with five.
1. And the vale of [Siddim](wiki:Battle_of_Siddim) *was* full of slime pits; and the kings of [Sodom and Gomorrah](wiki:Sodom_and_Gomorrah) fled, and fell there; and they that remained fled to the mountain.
1. And they took all the goods of [Sodom and Gomorrah](wiki:Sodom_and_Gomorrah), and all their victuals, and went their way.
1. And they took {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>`, {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`'s brother's son, who dwell in [Sodom](wiki:Sodom_and_Gomorrah), and his goods, and departed.
(GENESIS_14_13)=
13. And there came one that had escaped, and told {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` the Hebrew, for he dwelt in the plane of [Mamre](wiki:Mamre) the [Amorite](wiki:morites), brother of {ref}`Eshcol<WHO_Eshcol>`, and brother of {ref}`Aner<WHO_Aner>`: and these *were* confederates with {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`.
1. And when {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` heard that his brother was taken captive, he armed his trained *servants*, born in his own house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued *them* unto [Dan](wiki:Dan_(ancient_city)).
1. And he divided himself against them, he and his servants, by night, and smote them, and pursued them unto [Hobah](wiki:Hobah), which *is* on the left hand of [Damascus](wiki:Damascus).
1. And he brought back all the goods, and also brought again his brother {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>`, and his goods, and the women also, and the people.
