Joseph's eminence in [Pharaoh](wiki:Pharaoh)'s court, in which he served as the King's vice-regent (see {ref}`Gen. <GENESIS>` {ref}`41<GENESIS_41>`:{ref}`40<GENESIS_41_40>`-{ref}`45<GENESIS_41_45>`), is entirely consistent with what is known about the Semites in Egypt during the time of the patriarchs. Groups of West Semitic tribes, as part of the migrating people known as the [Hyksos](wiki:Hyksos) ("rulers of foreign lands"), descended upon [Egypt](wiki:Egypt) and, by the end of the 18th century B.C., seised control of the country, ruling it for several generations. the dagger handle reproduced here belogned to a high official of Semitic descent: "Nahman, who followed his lord" is the inscription. Discovered at [Sakkarah](wiki:Saqqara), in [Egypt](wiki:Egypt), the dagger handle (18th century B.C.) is made of what appears to be an alloy of gold and silver and has a hunting scene upon it.
