(GENESIS_13)=
# CHAPTER Thirteen, GENESIS

:::{div}
:class: paragraph-title
Abram and Lot Separate
:::

1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` went up out of [Egypt](wiki:Biblical_Egypt), he, and his wife, and all that he had, and {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` with him, into the south.
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` *was* very rich in cattle, in silver, and in gold.
1. And he went on his journeys from the south even to [Beth-el](wiki:Bethel), unto the place where his tent had been at the beginning, between [Beth-el](wiki:Bethel) and [Ha'i](wiki:Ai_(Canaan));
1. unto the place of the altar, which he had made there at the first: and there {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` called on the name of the {span .divine-name}`LORD`.
1. And {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` also, which went with {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`, had flocks, and herds, and tents.
1. And the land was not able to bear them, that they might dwell together: for their substance was great, so that they could not dwell together.
1. And there was a strife between the herdmen of {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`'s cattle and the herdmen of {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>`'s cattle: and the [Canaanite](wiki:Canaan) and the [Per'izzite](wiki:Perizzites) dwelt then in the land.
1. And {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` said unto {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>`, Let there be no strife, I pray thee, between me and thee, and between my herdmen and thy herdmen; for we *be* brethren.
1. *Is* not the whole land before thee? separate thyself, I pray thee, from me: if *thou wilt take* the left hand, then I will go to the right; or if *thou depart* to the right hand, then, I will go to the left.

10. And {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` lifted up his eyes, and beheld all the [plain of Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lot_(biblical_person)#The_plain_of_Jordan), that it *was* well watered every where, before the {span .divine-name}`LORD` destroyed [Sodom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodom_and_Gomorrah) and [Gomorrah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodom_and_Gomorrah), *even* as the garden of the {span .divine-name}`LORD`, like the land of [Egypt](wiki:Biblical_Egypt), as thou comest unto [Zo'ar](wiki:Zoara).
1. Then {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` chose him all the [plain of Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lot_(biblical_person)#The_plain_of_Jordan); and {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` journeyed east: and they separated themselves the one from the other.
1. {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` dwelt in the [land of Canaan](wiki:Canaan), and {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` dwelt in the cities of the plain, and pitched *his* tent toward [Sodom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodom_and_Gomorrah).
1. But the men of [Sodom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sodom_and_Gomorrah) *were* wicket and sinners before the {span .devine-name}`LORD` exceedingly.
1. And the {span .devine-name}`LORD` said unto {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>`, after that {ref}`Lot<WHO_Lot>` was separated from him, Life up now thine eyes, and look from the place where thou art northward, and southward, and eastward, and westward:
1. for all the land which thou seest, to thee will I give it, and to thy seed for ever.
1. And I will make thy seed as the dust of the earth: so that if a man can number the dust of the earth, *then* shall they seed also be numbered.
1. Arise, walk through the land in the length of it and in the breadth of it; for I will give it unto thee.
1. Then {ref}`Abram<WHO_Abram>` removed *his* tent, and come and dwelt in the plain of [Mamre](wiki:Mamre), which *is* in [Hebron](wiki:Hebron), and built there an altar unto the {span .divine-name}`LORD`.
