{ref}`Jehu<WHO_Jehu>` ascented the thrown of Isreal when the Assyrian forces of [King Shalmaneser III](wiki:Shalmaneser_III) were sweeping southward through [Syria](wiki:Syria_(region)). In an attempt to save Israel from conquest, {ref}`Jehu<WHO_Jehu>` hastened to pay tribute to [Shalmaneser](wiki:Shalmaneser_III). On an obelisk found at [Calah](wiki:Nimrud), {ref}`King Jehu<WHO_Jehu>` is shown kneeling at inscription mentiones "Jehu the son of Omri" (i.e., King "of the House of Omri", the Assyrian name for Israel), and lists the items of gold, silver, and copper that were brought by {ref}`Jehu<WHO_Jehu>` as tribute. The widespread conquests of [Shalmaneser III](wiki:Shalmaneser_III) are depicted on the bronze doors of his palace at [Balawat](wiki:Balawat) shown {ref}`below<fig:Door_fitting_Balawat_gate>`.
