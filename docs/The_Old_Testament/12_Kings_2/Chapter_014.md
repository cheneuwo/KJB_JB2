Personal names recorded in the Bible have been found on a number of seals exavated in [Palestine](wiki:Palestine_(region)). The seal at the right is that of a royal minister of {ref}`Jeroboam<WHO_Jeroboam>` in the 8th century B.C. It was found at [Megiddo](wiki:Tel_Megiddo) and is skillfully engraved with the figure of a roaring lion. The inscription, "[belonging] to Shema the servant of Jeroboam", proclaims the name and rank of its owner, a minister of [King Jeroboam II](wiki:Jeroboam_II) (784-748 B.C.). The word "servant" here has the connotation of minister and high dignitary, as in the title "the servant of the king", which has also been found engraved on the seals of the same period.
