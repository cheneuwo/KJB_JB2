[Ur](wiki:Ur), a leading city of the [Sumerian civilization](wiki:Sumer), is the source of one of the most important objects ever found: the [Standard of Ur](wiki:Standard_of_Ur) war panel (about 2500 B.C.), which unfolds a panorama of the Sumerian army. The upper register shows the king (centre, holding scepter) receiving a file of bound captives; he is followed by the royal guard and his four-wheeled war chariot drawn by horses. In the middle register, a phalanx of soldiers, armed with spears and wearing metal helmets and studded capes, charges the enermy. The lower register is filled with a battle charge of chariots (some authorities interpret the scene as the same chariot in successive stages of motion). The designs are made with shell, lapid lazuli, and red limestone. The panel is an incomparable picture of the military might that gave the Sumerians dominance over Mesopotamia for hundreds of years. ([Image courtesy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Standard_of_Ur_-_War.jpg), Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons. Accessed on October 24, 2026.)
