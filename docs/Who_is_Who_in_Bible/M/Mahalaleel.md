# Mahal'aleel

(The name *Mahal'aleel* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`:{ref}`12<GENESIS_5_12>`)

Mahal'aleel was the son of {ref}`Ca-i'nan<WHO_Cainan>`, born when {ref}`Ca-i'nan<WHO_Cainan>` was {ref}`70 years old<GENESIS_5_12>`. Mahal'aleel fathered {ref}`Jared<WHO_Jared>` when he was {ref}`65 years old <GENESIS_5_16>`. Mahal'aleel also fathered {ref}`other sons and daughters<GENESIS_5_16>`. {ref}`Mahal'aleel died at the age of 895<GENESIS_5_17>`.
