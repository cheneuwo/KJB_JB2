# Magog

(The name *Magog* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`2<GENESIS_10_2>`, referring to the Magog, son of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>`)

Magog is the second of the seven sons of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>` mentioned in the [Table of Nations](wiki:Generations_of_Noah) in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`.

Magog is often associated with [apocalyptic](wiki:Apocalypticism) traditions, mainly in connection with {ref}`Ezekiel<EZEKIEL>` {ref}`38<EZEKIEL_38>` and {ref}`39<EZEKIEL_39>` which mentions "{ref}`Gog of the land of Magog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal<EZEKIEL_38_2>`" ({ref}`Ezek<EZEKIEL>` {ref}`38<EZEKIEL_38>`:{ref}`2<EZEKIEL_38_2>`); on the basis of this mention, "Gog and Magog" over time became associated with each other as a pair. In the New Testament, this pairing is found in the {ref}`Book of Revelation<REVELATION>` {ref}`20<REVELATION_20>`:{ref}`8<REVELATION_20_8>`, in which instance they may merely be metaphors for archetypal enemies of God.
