# Methu'selah

(muh thoo' zuh lun) HEBREW: METHUSELAH

*"javelin man" or "worshiper of the diety"*

(The name *Mathu'selah* first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`:{ref}`21<GENESIS_5_21>`)

Methu'selah is a biblical [patriarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriarchs_(Bible)) mentioned in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`:{ref}`21<GENESIS_5_21>`-{ref}`27<GENESIS_5_27>`, as part of the genealogy linking {ref}`Adam<WHO_Adam>` to {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`. Methu'selah was a descendant of {ref}`Seth<WHO_Seth>`, the son of {ref}`Enoch<WHO_Enoch_son_of_Jared>`, the father of {ref}`Lamech<WHO_Lamech_son_of_Methuselah>`, and the grandfather of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`. Elsewhere in the Bible, Meshu'selah is mentioned in genealogies in {ref}`1 Chronicles<CHRONICLES_1_1_3>` and the {ref}`Gospel of Luke<LUKE_3_37>`.

Based on {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>`:{ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`,{ref}`21<GENESIS_5_21>` and {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>`:{ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`,{ref}`25<GENESIS_5_25>`.


Most often recalled for his extraordinary longevity and fertility. Becoming the father of {ref}`Lamech<WHO_Lamech_son_of_Methuselah>` at {ref}`the age of 187<GENESIS_5_25>`, Methu'selah lived {ref}`another 782 years and had other sons and daughters<GENESIS_5_26>`. "{ref}`and and all the days of Methu'selah were nine hundred sixty and nine years<GENESIS_5_27>`" ({ref}`Gen<GENESIS>`. {ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`:{ref}`27<GENESIS_5_27>`), making him the longest-lived person in the Bible.

However, when his lifespan is considered in the context of the prevailing Babylonian tradition in which ancestors were said to have lived for tens of thousands of years, Methu'selah spent a comparatively short time on earth. He did not live even the thousand years that, according to {ref}`Psalms<Psalms>` {ref}`90<Psalms_90>`:{ref}`4<Psalms_90_4>`, were but a single day in the life of the LORD. Methu'selah died in the year of the Flood.
