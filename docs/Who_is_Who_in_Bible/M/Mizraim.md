# Miz'raim

(The name *Miz'raim* first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`6<GENESIS_10_6>`, referring to the Miz'raim, son of {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>`, grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`).

According to {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`6<GENESIS_10_6>`, [Mizraim](wiki:Mizraim), son of {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>`, was the younger brother of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>` and elder brother of {ref}`Phut<WHO_Phut>` and {ref}`Canaan<WHO_Canaan>`, whose families together made up the [Hamite](wiki:Ham_(Genesis)) branch of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`'s descendants. Some translations, such as the [English Standard Version](wiki:English_Standard_Version), refer to Mizraim as "[Egypt](wiki:Egypt)".

Mizraim's sons where {ref}`Ludim<WHO_Ludim>`, {ref}`An'amim<WHO_Anamin>`, {ref}`Le'habim<WHO_Lehabim>`, {ref}`Naph'tuhim<WHO_Naphtuhim>`, {ref}`Path'rusim<WHO_Pathrusim>`, {ref}`Cas'luhim<WHO_Casluhim>` and {ref}`Caph'torim<WHO_Caphtorim>`.
