# Miz'ra-im

(The name *Miz'ra-im* first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`6<GENESIS_10_6>`, referring to the Miz'ra-im, son of {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>`, grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`).

According to {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`6<GENESIS_10_6>`, [Miz'ra-im](wiki:Mizraim), son of {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>`, was the younger brother of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>` and elder brother of {ref}`Phut<WHO_Phut>` and {ref}`Canaan<WHO_Canaan>`, whose families together made up the [Hamite](wiki:Ham_(Genesis)) branch of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`'s descendants. Some translations, such as the [English Standard Version](wiki:English_Standard_Version), refer to Mizraim as "[Egypt](wiki:Egypt)".
