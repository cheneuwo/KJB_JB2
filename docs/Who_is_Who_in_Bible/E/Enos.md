# Enos

(eh-NOHSH) HEBREW:

"*mortal man*"

(The name *Enos*  first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`26<GENESIS_4_26>`)

Enos was the son of {ref}`Seth<WHO_Seth>`, born when {ref}`Seth<WHO_Seth>` was {ref}`105 years old<GENESIS_5_6>`. Enos fathered {ref}`Ca-i'nan<WHO_Cainan>` when he was {ref}`90 years old<GENESIS_5_9>`. Enos also fathered {ref}`other sons and daughters<GENESIS_5_10>`. {ref}`Enos died at the age of 905<GENESIS_5_11>`.

Enos, or *Enosh* depending on translation, is a figure in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>`. He is described as the first son of {ref}`Seth<WHO_Seth>` who figures in the {ref}`Generations of Adam<GENESIS_5_1>`, and is also referred to within the genealogies of {ref}`1 Chronicles<CHRONICLES_1>`.

He is part of the [genealogy of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genealogy_of_Jesus) as mentioned in {ref}`Luke<LUKE>` {ref}`3<LUKE_3>`:{ref}`38<LUKE_3_38>`.
