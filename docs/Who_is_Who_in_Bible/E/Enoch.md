# Enoch, son of Cain

(e'nok) HEBREW: HANOK

(The name *Enoch* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`17<GENESIS_4_17>`, referring to the Enoch, son of {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`)

The name *Enoch* refers to multiple biblical figures, in order of appearence:
- **Enoch** in this article, son of {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`, a descendant of {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`, first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`17<GENESIS_4_17>`, and
- {ref}`Enoch<WHO_Enoch_son_of_Jared>`, son of {ref}`Jared<WHO_Jared>`, a descendant of {ref}`Seth<WHO_Seth>`, first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`:{ref}`18<GENESIS_5_18>`.

In {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`17<GENESIS_4_17>`-{ref}`18<GENESIS_4_18>`, Enoch is a descendant of {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`, the son of {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`, and the father of {ref}`Irad<WHO_Irad>`.

{ref}`After Cain arrived<GENESIS_4_16>` in the [Land of Nod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_of_Nod), to which he was banished by God as his punishment for {ref}`murdering his brother<GENESIS_4_8>` {ref}`Abel<WHO_Abel>`, his wife became pregnant and bore {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`'s first child, {ref}`whom he named Enoch<GENESIS_4_17>`.

After the birth of Enoch, {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>` {ref}`build a city and named it after his son<GENESIS_4_17>`.
