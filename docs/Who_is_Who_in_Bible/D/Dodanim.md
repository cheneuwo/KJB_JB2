# Do'danim

(The name *Do'danim* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`4<GENESIS_10_4>`, referring to the Do'danim, son of {ref}`Gomer<WHO_Gomer>`, grandson of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>`).

Do'danim is one of the four named sons of {ref}`Javan<WHO_Javan>`, a son of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>` and a grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`. Scripture does not name any descendants of Tarshish.
