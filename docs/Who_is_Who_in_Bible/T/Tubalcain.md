# Tu'bal-cain

HEBREW: תּוּבַל קַיִן

(The name *Tubalcain* first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`22<GENESIS_4_22>`)

Tubal-cain, or Tubalcain, is a descendant of {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`, son of {ref}`Lamech<WHO_Lamech>`, brother of {ref}`Naamah<WHO_Naamah>` and half-brother of {ref}`Jabal<WHO_Jabal>` and {ref}`Jubal<WHO_Jubal>`. His mother is {ref}`Zillah<WHO_Zillah>`.
