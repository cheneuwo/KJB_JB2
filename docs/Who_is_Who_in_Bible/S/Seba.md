# Seba

(The name *Seba* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`7<GENESIS_10_7>`, referring to the Seba, son of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>`, great-grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`.)

[Seba](wiki:seba) was the son of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>` according to {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`7<GENESIS_10_7>` and {ref}`1 Chronicles<CHRONICLES_1>` {ref}`1<CHRONICLES_1_1>`:{ref}`9<CHRONICLES_1_1_9>`, and also by extension of the name of a people and region mentioned in {ref}`Isaiah<ISAIAH>` {ref}`43<ISAIAH_43>`:{ref}`3<ISAIAH_43_3>`, {ref}`Isaiah<ISAIAH>` {ref}`45<ISAIAH_45>`:{ref}`14<ISAIAH_45_14>` and {ref}`Psalm<Psalms>` {ref}`72<Psalms_72>`:{ref}`10<Psalms_72_10>`.

 {ref}`Psalm<Psalms>` {ref}`72<Psalms_72>`:{ref}`10<Psalms_72_10>` and {ref}`Isaiah<ISAIAH>` {ref}`43<ISAIAH_43>`:{ref}`3<ISAIAH_43_3>` both imply that Seba was a wealthy kingdom. The land of Seba is usually located in South Arabia or the Horn of Africa. On balance, the biblical evidence points to an African location. Both passages in <ref>`Isaiah<ISAIAH>` associate it with Egypt and {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>`.
