# Seba

(The name *Seba* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`7<GENESIS_10_7>`, referring to the Seba, son of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>`, great-grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`.)
