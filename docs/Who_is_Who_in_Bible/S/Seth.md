# Seth

HEBREW: SHET

(The name *Seth* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`25<GENESIS_4_25>`)

Seth was the son of {ref}`Adam<WHO_Adam>` and {ref}`Eve<WHO_Eve>`, born when {ref}`Adam<WHO_Adam>` was {ref}`130 years old<GENESIS_5_3>`. Seth fathered {ref}`Enos<WHO_Enos>` when he was {ref}`105 years old<GENESIS_5_6>`. Seth also fathered {ref}`other sons and daughters<GENESIS_5_7>`. {ref}`Seth died at the age of 912<GENESIS_5_8>`.

After {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>` killed his brother {ref}`Abel<WHO_Abel>` and was banished, {ref}`Eve<WHO_Eve>` bore another son to her husband, {ref}`Adam<WHO_Adam>`. She called him Seth, saying, "{ref}`God has appointed for me another child instead of Abel, for Cain slew him<GENESIS_4_25>`" ({ref}`Gen.<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`25<GENESIS_4_25>`). {ref}`At the age of 105, Seth sired a son<GENESIS_5_6>`, {ref}`Enos<WHO_Enos>`, and {ref}`lived another 807 years<GENESIS_5_7>`. The image of God that was conferred upon the human race in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`1<GENESIS_1>`:{ref}`26<GENESIS_1_26>`-{ref}`27<GENESIS_1_27>` was transmitted through the line of Seth; his descendant **{ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`** was the ancestor of all humanity after the Flood. During the life-time of Seth and {ref}`Enos<WHO_Enos>`, "{ref}`men began to call upon the name of the Loard<GENESIS_4_26>`" ({ref}`Gen.<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`26<GENESIS_4_26>`).
