# Sheba, son of Ra'amah

(The name *Sabtah* first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`7<GENESIS_10_7>`, referring to the Sheba, son of {ref}`Ra'amah<WHO_Raamah>`, descendant of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` via {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>`.)

The name *Sheba* refers to multiple biblical figures, in order of appearence:
- **Sheba** in this article, son of {ref}`Ra'amah<WHO_Raamah>`, descendant of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` via {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>`, and
- {ref}`Sheba<WHO_Sheba_son_of_Joktan>`, descendant of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` via {ref}`Shem<WHO_Shem>`, first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`28<GENESIS_10_28>`.
