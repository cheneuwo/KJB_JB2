Ham was a son of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` in Old Testament and the father of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>`, {ref}`Mizraim<WHO_Mizraim>`, {ref}`Phut<WHO_Phut>`, and {ref}`Canaan<WHO_Canaan>`, (Published by [Guillaume Rouille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillaume_Rouill%C3%A9) (1518?-1589), Public domain, [Image courtesy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ham02.jpg), via **Wikimedia Commons**, Accesses on October 12, 2025.)

Although he is always listed between his two brothers, {ref}`Shem<WHO_Shem>` and {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>`, Ham was apparently the youngest son of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`. All three brothers and their wives accompanied their father on the ark. After the floodwaters receded, {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` planted a vineyard, and one day {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>` came upon his father naked and intoxicated in his tent. He told his brothers of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`'s condition, and they carefully covered their father, walking backward so as not to see his body, since Israelite tradition prohibited viewing the nakedness of a parent.

When {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` recovered from his drunkenness and realized what had happened, he said, "{ref}`cursed be Canaan; a slave of slaves shall he be to his brothers<GENESIS_9_25>`" ({ref}`Gen<GENESIS>`. {ref}`9<GENESIS_9>`:{ref}`25<GENESIS_9_25>`). Interpreters differ as to the reason why {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` cursed **{ref}`Canaan<WHO_Canaan>`**, who was one of Ham's sons, rather than Ham himself. However, in the Bible it is not uncommon for children to be punished for the sins of their parents. Some of {ref}`Canaan<WHO_Canaan>`'s descendants, the [Gibeonites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibeon_(ancient_city)) -- in line, apparently, with {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`'s prophecy -- were told by **{ref}`Joshua<WHO_Joshua>`**, "{ref}`You shall always be slaves, hewers of wood and drawers of water<JOSHUA_9_23>`" ({ref}`Jos<JOSHUA>`. {ref}`9<JOSHUA_9>`:{ref}`23<JOSHUA_9_23>`).

Ham's sons were {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>`, {ref}`Mizraim<WHO_Mizraim>`, {ref}`Phut<WHO_Phut>`, and {ref}`Canaan<WHO_Canaan>`. The descendants of three -- the [Cushites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kushites), the [Egyptians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptians), and the [Canaanites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaan#Canaanites) -- figure prominently in the Bible. {ref}`Phut<WHO_Phut>` apparently engendered the people of either Lybia or the Horn of Africa, areas that are mentioned less often in the Scriptures.
