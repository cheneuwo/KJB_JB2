# Hav'ilah, son of Cush

(The name *Hav'ilah* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`7<GENESIS_10_7>`, referring to the Hav'ilah, son of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>`, great-grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`).

The name *Hav'ilah* refers to multiple biblical figures, in order of appearence:
- **Hav'ilah** in this article, son of {ref}`Cush<WHO_Cush>`, great-grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`, and
- {ref}`Hav'ilah<WHO_Havilah_son_of_Joktan>`, descendant of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>` via {ref}`Shem<WHO_Shem>`, first appeared in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`29<GENESIS_10_29>`.
