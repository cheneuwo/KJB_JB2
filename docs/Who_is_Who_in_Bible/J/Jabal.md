# Jabal

(yah-bawl) HEBREW: YABAL

(The name *Jabal* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`20<GENESIS_4_20>`)

Jabal, a descedant of {ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`, was the sun of {ref}`Lamech<WHO_Lamech>` and {ref}`Adah<WHO_Adah>`, and the brother of {ref}`Jubal<WHO_Jubal>`, half-brother of {ref}`Tu'bal-cain<WHO_Tubalcain>` and {ref}`Na'amah<WHO_Naamah>`. He is described as the "{ref}`ancestor of all who live in tents and raise livestock<GENESIS_4_20>`." ({ref}`Gen.<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`20<GENESIS_4_20>`)
