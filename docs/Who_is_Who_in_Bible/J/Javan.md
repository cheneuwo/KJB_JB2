# Javan

(The name *Javan* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`2<GENESIS_10_2>`, referring to the Javan, son of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>`)

Javan was the fourth son of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>` and a grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`. The traditional belief is that Javan was the ancestor of the Greeks.
