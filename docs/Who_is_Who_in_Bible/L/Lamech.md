Based on {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`1<GENESIS_4_1>`, {ref}`2<GENESIS_4_2>`, {ref}`17<GENESIS_4_17>`--{ref}`22<GENESIS_4_22>`, and {ref}`25<GENESIS_4_25>`.

A fifth-generation descendant of **{ref}`Cain<WHO_Cain>`**, Lamech was the first biblical figure to have two wives, {ref}`Adah<WHO_Adah>` and {ref}`Zillah<WHO_Zillah>`. The women bore three sons: {ref}`Jabal<WHO_Jabal>`, whose descendants were "{ref}`those who dwell in tents and have cattle<GENESIS_4_20>`" ({ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`20<GENESIS_4_20>`); {ref}`Jubal<WHO_Jubal>`, who "{ref}`was the father of all those who play the lyre and pipe<GENESIS_4_21>`" ({ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`21<GENESIS_4_21>`); and {ref}`Tu'bal-cain<WHO_Tubalcain>`, "{ref}`the forger of all instruments of bronze and iron<GENESIS_4_22>`" ({ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`22<GENESIS_4_22>`). These instruments were, in fact, weapons as well as tools. They marked another step in mankind's decline, illustrated by Lamech's boastful song: "{ref}`I have slain a man for wounding me, a young man for striking me. If Cain is avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech [is avenged] seventy-sevenfold<GENESIS_4_23>`"  ({ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`23<GENESIS_4_23>`-{ref}`24<GENESIS_4_24>`). The sins of pride and vengenfulness and the glorification of killing were thus added to the list of human failing.
