Based on {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>`:{ref}`5<GENESIS_5>`,{ref}`32<GENESIS_5_32>` and {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>`:{ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`,{ref}`6<GENESIS_10_6>`.

The son of {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>` and grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`, Canaan was the ancestor of the [Canaanites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaan), a loose grouping of peoples who proved troublesome to the [Israelites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelites) during the period of settlement in the [Promised Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promised_Land). It was {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>` who "{ref}`saw the nakedness<GENESIS_9_22>`" of the drunken {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`, but Canaan who received the curse: "{ref}`a slave of slaves shall he be to his brothers<GENESIS_9_25>`" ({ref}`Gen.<GENESIS>` {ref}`9<GENESIS_9>`:{ref}`22<GENESIS_9_22>`, {ref}`25<GENESIS_9_25>`).

The curse seemed to have been realized when the [Israelites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelites) "{ref}`put the Cannanites to forced labor<JUDGES_1_28>`". Among the descendants of Canaan listed in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`15<GENESIS_10_15>` where Heth (the [Hittites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hittites)), the [Jebusites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jebusites) (a people who lived in the vicinity of Jerusalem), and [Amorites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amorites) (people of the Palestinian hill country), and the [Hivites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hivites) (perhaps the [Horites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horites) or [Hurrians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurrians)).
