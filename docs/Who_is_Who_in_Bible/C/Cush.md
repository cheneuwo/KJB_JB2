# Cush

(The name *Cush* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`6<GENESIS_10_6>`, referring to the Cush, son of {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>`, grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`).

[Cush](wiki:Cush_(Bible)), or **Kush**, according to the Hebrew Bible, was the oldest son of {ref}`Ham<WHO_Ham>` and a grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`. He was the brother of {ref}`Miz'raim<WHO_Mizraim>`, {ref}`Phut<WHO_Phut>`, and {ref}`Canaan<WHO_Canaan>`. Cush was the father of {ref}`Seba<WHO_Seba>`, and {ref}`Hav'ilah<WHO_Havilah>`, and {ref}`Sabtah<WHO_Sabtah>`, and {ref}`Ra'amah<WHO_Raamah>` and {ref}`Sab'techa<WHO_Sabtecha>` and  {ref}`Nimrod<WHO_Nimrod>`.
