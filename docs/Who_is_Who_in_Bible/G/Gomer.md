# Gomer

(The name *Gomer* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`2<GENESIS_10_2>`, referring to the Gomer, son of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>`)

[Gomer](wiki:Gomer) was the eldest son of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>`, and father of {ref}`Ash'kenaz<WHO_Ashkenaz>`, {ref}`Riphath<WHO_Riphath>`, and {ref}`Togar'mah<WHO_Togarmah>`, according to the [Table of Nations](wiki:Generations_of_Noah) in Hebrew Bible  ({ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`).
