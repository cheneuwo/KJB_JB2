(WHO_Adah)=
# Adah

(aw-daw') HEBREW: עֵדָה , Modern: Ada

*"ornament"*

1. The first wife of {ref}`Lamech<WHO_Lamech>`, and the mother of {ref}`Jabal<WHO_Jabal>` and {ref}`Jubal<WHO_Jubal>` ({ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`:{ref}`19<GENESIS_4_19>`--{ref}`23<GENESIS_4_23>`)
1. The first wife of {ref}`Esau<WHO_Esau>`, the daughter of Elon the [Hittite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblical_Hittites). It has been suggested by biblical scholars that she is the same person as "{ref}`Bash-emath the daughter of Elon the Hittite<GENESIS_26_34>`", mentioned as the wife of {ref}`Esau<WHO_Esau>` in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`26<GENESIS_26>`. She bore {ref}`Esau<WHO_Esau>`'s firstborn {ref}`Eliphaz<WHO_Eliphaz>`, and became the matriarch of the [Endomites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edom).

This article is about the first wife of {ref}`Lamech<WHO_Lamech>` as described in {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`4<GENESIS_4>`.
