# Kittim

(The name *Kittim* first appeared in  {ref}`Genesis<GENESIS>` {ref}`10<GENESIS_10>`:{ref}`4<GENESIS_10_4>`, referring to the Kittim, son of {ref}`Gomer<WHO_Gomer>`, grandson of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>`.)

Kittim is one of the four named sons of {ref}`Javan<WHO_Javan>`, a son of {ref}`Japheth<WHO_Japheth>` and a grandson of {ref}`Noah<WHO_Noah>`. Scripture does not name any descendants of Tarshish.
