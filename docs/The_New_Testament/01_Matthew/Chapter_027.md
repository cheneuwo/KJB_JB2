Pictured here is one of the most interesting archaeological discoveries yet made bearing on a person of the New Testament. It is a fragmentary inscription in Latin on a stone found at [Caesarea](wiki:Caesarea_Maritima) in 1961. It is dedicated to the [Emperor Tiberius](wiki:Tiberius) by "[Pon]tius Pilate, prefect of Judea". Pilate served as the governor of [Judea](wiki:Judea) from 26 to 36 A.D. His official residence was at [Caesarea](wiki:Caesarea_Maritima). He was removed from the governorship several years after the death of {ref}`Jesus<WHO_Jesus>`.
