Siloam was a section of Jerusalem around the [Pool of Siloam](wiki:Pool_of_Siloam) (see map here), mentioned in {ref}`John<JOHN>` {ref}`9<JOHN_9>`:{ref}`7<JOHN_9_7>`,{ref}`11<JOHN_9_11>`. The exact location of the tower to which {ref}`Jesus<WHO_Jesus>` referred is not known. The original name of the small reservoir was the Pool of Shiloah. In {ref}`Nehemiah<NEHEMIAH>` {ref}`3<NEHEMIAH_3>`:{ref}`15<NEHEMIAH_3_15>` it is called "the pool of Siloah by the King's garden". The Hebrew word "shiloah" means a "sending" of water, that is, a conduit. At the time of [King Ahaz](wiki:Ahaz) (735-715 B.C.) "the conduit of the upper pool" ({ref}`Is.<ISAIAH>` {ref}7<ISAIAH_7>`:{ref}`3<ISAIAH_7_3>`)
