These verses reflect the custom -- still practiced by Jews today -- of including in the synagogue service a reading from the prophetic books of the Bible. The reading was performed by a member of the congregation, and {ref}`Luke<WHO_Luke>` here relates how {ref}`Jesus<WHO_Jesus>`, a member of the [Nazareth](wiki:Nazareth) congregation, read aloud {ref}`chapter 61<ISAIAH_61>`, verses {ref}`1<ISAIAH_61_1>`-{ref}`2<ISAIAH_61_2>` of {ref}`Isaiah<ISAIAH>`. Among the [Dead Sea Scrolls](wiki:Dead_Sea_Scrolls) was found the earliest-known complete [scroll of Isaiah](wiki:Isaiah_Scroll), a part of which appears here.
